Critter Stack Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026 · Published by Wood Interactive (Matthew Wood)

The short version

  • There's no Critter Stack account. We don't ask for your email, phone number or real name.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device. We can't see it.
  • Leaderboards and achievements run through Apple's Game Center, and purchases through the App Store.
  • The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Ads are only personalized if you tap "Allow" when your iPhone asks about tracking. Buying No Ads turns ads off.
  • We don't sell your personal information.

1. What's saved on your device

To remember your game, Critter Stack stores the following on your iPhone or iPad only:

  • Game progress: critters sent home, unlocked animals and accessories, stickers, medals, streaks, and your skip and undo balance.
  • Your local high scores and Daily stack results.
  • Settings, such as sound, dark mode, board frame, your crest critter and whether reminders are on.
  • The player name you type. It's shown on your own screens and isn't sent to us. Names are checked on the device to keep them friendly.

This information never leaves your device except through the Apple services described below. Deleting the app deletes it.

2. Game Center

Leaderboards (daily and weekly rankings) and achievements use Apple's Game Center. When you're signed in to Game Center, the app sends Apple:

  • Your scores for each board, and the achievements you earn.
  • A small number attached to each score that shows your crest critter and medal count, so other players see your badge next to your score.

Other players see your Game Center nickname, your scores, and that badge. Apple runs Game Center under Apple's Privacy Policy, and you control your Game Center profile and visibility in iPhone Settings › Game Center. We use your rank on finished boards only to award podium prizes (skips, undos and medals) inside the game.

3. In-app purchases

Purchases (skip, undo and life packs, an hour of unlimited lives, No Ads, the zoo unlock and the bundle) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment card or billing details. The app receives a signed confirmation from Apple of what was bought, so it can unlock the item and let you use Restore purchases. Apple's Privacy Policy and the App Store terms apply.

4. Advertising

The free version of Critter Stack shows occasional full-screen ads between games, supplied by Google AdMob (Google LLC). Ads start only after your first 8 games. You can also choose to watch a short ad to earn a free life, skip or undo (up to 5 a day); those are always optional. Players in the EU and UK are asked for consent first and can change it any time in Settings › Ad privacy choices. To choose and show ads, count views and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may collect:

Tracking permission. Before the first ad, your iPhone asks whether Critter Stack may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you tap Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier isn't shared and you'll see ads that aren't personalized. The game works exactly the same either way. You can change this anytime in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Family-friendly ads. We ask AdMob to show only ads suitable for general audiences.

No Ads. Buying No Ads or the Everything bundle turns ads off permanently.

Google's use of this information is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Apple's SKAdNetwork may also report anonymous, aggregated ad-install counts without identifying you.

5. Reminders

Daily reminders are optional and off until you turn them on. They're scheduled on your device; we don't run a notification server and don't receive anything when you allow them. You can turn them off in the game's Settings or in iPhone Settings › Notifications.

6. What we don't collect

  • No account, email address, phone number or real name.
  • No precise location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health data.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services of our own.
  • We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it with anyone except as described above (Apple for Game Center and purchases, and Google for ads).

7. Children

Critter Stack is made for a general audience and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no chat, no way to message other players, and no sign-up. If you're a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the app, contact us at the address below and we'll help. On a child's device, you can use Apple's Screen Time and Ask to Buy to manage purchases, and turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your game data: delete the app.
  • Personalized ads: choose Ask App Not to Track, or turn off tracking in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. You can also reset your advertising identifier in iOS settings. Or buy No Ads.
  • Game Center: manage or sign out of your profile in iPhone Settings › Game Center.
  • Reminders: turn them off in the game or in iOS notification settings.

If you're in the EU, UK or a similar region, you have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we don't hold personal data on our own servers, most requests are best handled with the controls above or directly with Apple or Google. We'll still help with any request sent to us. Where ads are personalized, the legal basis is your consent (the tracking prompt). Keeping your game working relies on our legitimate interest in providing the app you asked for.

If you're in California or another US state with a privacy law, note that personalized advertising can count as "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can opt out by choosing Ask App Not to Track or turning tracking off in iOS settings, and we honor that choice. We don't sell personal information for money, and we don't knowingly sell or share information of consumers under 16.

9. How long data is kept

Game data on your device stays until you delete the app. Game Center scores and achievements are kept by Apple under its policies. Advertising data is kept by Google as described in its policies.

10. Where data is processed

Apple and Google may process information in the United States and other countries where they operate, using the safeguards described in their privacy policies. We (Wood Interactive (Matthew Wood)) are based in Virginia, United States.

11. Changes to this policy, and how to contact us

If we change how Critter Stack handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
