Critter Stack Help

Stuck, lost a purchase, or found a bug? Here are quick answers, and a real person if you need one.

Contact us support@woodinteractive.app
We usually reply within 2 business days. Please include your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.

Purchases

I bought No Ads, the zoo or the bundle, but it's gone

Open Settings (the gear) or the Store and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy. Restoring brings back No Ads and the zoo unlock. Skip, undo and life packs are used up as you play, so they can't be restored.

I was charged but didn't get my skips, undos, turns or lives

Close Critter Stack completely and open it again. Purchases that finish in the background are delivered on the next launch. If they still don't arrive, email us with the date of the purchase (you'll find it in your Apple receipt).

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments, so refunds go through Apple: visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the Critter Stack purchase.

How do lives work?

Every game uses one life. You can hold 5, and one comes back every 20 minutes. Your first Daily Stack try each day is free, and solving a puzzle gives its life back. Tap the ❤️ on the main menu to see when the next one arrives, watch a short ad for a free life (up to 5 free rewards a day), or get a refill or an hour of unlimited play in the Store.

Your progress

I got a new phone. Can I bring my zoo?

In this version your zoo, stickers and medals are saved on the device you play on, so they don't move to a new phone yet. Leaderboard scores and achievements are kept by Game Center, and Restore purchases brings back No Ads and the zoo unlock on any device. Moving progress between devices is planned for a future update.

How do I start over?

Delete Critter Stack and install it again. Your purchases come back with Restore purchases.

Daily Stack and leaderboards

When does the Daily Stack reset?

At midnight UTC, which is 8 PM US Eastern during daylight saving time and 7 PM otherwise. The game shows a countdown on the menu and the Rankings screen. Everyone in the world plays the same 100 critters until the reset.

Why can't I use skips or undos in the Daily Stack?

To keep it fair. The Daily Stack is pure skill, with three tries per stack and your best try counting. Skips and undos work in every other mode.

I don't see myself on the leaderboard
  • Check that you're signed in to Game Center (iPhone Settings › Game Center).
  • Scores set while offline are sent automatically the next time you open the game online.
  • Leaderboards show your Game Center nickname, not the name you typed in the game.
How do podium prizes and medals work?

Finish in the top 3 of a Daily Stack or a weekly board (Petting Zoo, Challenge or Critter Pen) and you win skips, undos and a permanent medal for your nameplate. Daily prizes are bigger. Prizes arrive the next time you open the game after the board closes.

Everything else

How do I turn off sounds, music, vibration or reminders?

Tap the gear on the main screen. Every sound, the music, haptics and the daily reminder have their own switch. Reminders can also be turned off in iPhone Settings › Notifications › Critter Stack.

Too much motion or confetti?

Turn on Reduce Motion in iPhone Settings › Accessibility › Motion. Critter Stack then turns off screen shake, confetti and bouncing animations.
