    Critter Pen
    
      
    Drag any of the three critters into the pen. Fill a row or a column and everyone in it goes home.
    
      
    Tap a critter twice to spend a ↻ Turn. It stays unlocked until you place it, so tap it to spin it as often as you like.
    
      
    Clear several lines in one move for more points. Keep clearing move after move to build a combo. Three in a row starts Fever: the board glows and clears score extra until a move clears nothing. When none of your three fit, the pen is full.
